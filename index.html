sample workspace · fictional Caldbeck X200/X250 · Service Manual Rev 4 (invented)

Asked: Customer has an X250 throwing E04 and the flow looks normal

Short answer

Check the ship date first. X250 units shipped before 2026-03-15 carry a known sensor drift fault that shows a false E04. Normal flow with a low-side E04 on one of those units points to the sensor. On later units, E04 usually means a clogged pre-filter.

Steps

  1. Confirm the model from the serial plate. E04 exists on the X250 only.
  2. Record the ship date from the serial plate.
  3. Hold the mode button for 5 seconds to reach the diagnostics screen.
  4. Note whether the pressure reading is high-side (above 62 PSI) or low-side (below 18 PSI).
  5. If the reading is low-side and the ship date is before 2026-03-15, stop diagnosing. Follow the known-issue doc.
  6. If the reading is high-side, ask whether output flow has fallen over recent weeks.
  7. Have the customer inspect the pre-filter cartridge. A loaded cartridge is discolored across its full depth.
  8. If the cartridge is loaded, replace it and clear the code. The unit clears E04 after 60 seconds of in-range readings.
  9. If the code returns within 24 hours of a cartridge replacement, escalate. Do not replace a second cartridge.

Escalate if

  • E04 returns within 24 hours of a pre-filter replacement. Product engineering.
  • The diagnostics screen shows a reading inside the 18 to 62 PSI range while E04 is displayed. Product engineering.

E04 fires when the control board reads inlet-side pressure above 62 PSI or below 18 PSI for more than 30 continuous seconds. The display shows the code; it does not show which direction. That distinction is on the diagnostics screen, reached by holding the mode button for 5 seconds.

The X200 does not have this fault and does not show error codes at all. It has a mechanical gauge and no control board.

Asked: Customer's unit shows code 49 and the bottom outdoor fan isn't spinning

Code 49 is an outdoor DC fan motor fault on the lower fan. The manual lists five causes, in order: loose wiring, failed cord, failed motor, failed drive circuit, blocked fan. Start by checking the fan spins freely, then reconnect the wiring.

troubleshooting/outdoor-main-control-fault-codes § Details · pp. 53–56

Escalate if: the drive board fault LED is lit. That's a different table.

Code 18 can be normal protection. It fires when AC voltage is above 275 V or below 160 V. The manual's first documented action is “normal protection, check the supply power” before replacing anything.

troubleshooting/outdoor-main-control-fault-codes § Details · pp. 53–55

Note: codes 5, 16, 47 and 91 also list normal protection as a documented outcome.

Not in the knowledge base. The manual doesn't state which capacities have two outdoor fans, and I won't infer it from board diagrams. I've filed this as a gap for product engineering.

Gap #14 filed · 1st ask · routed to product engineering

Hi Dana,

Code 49 means the unit's lower outdoor fan motor has stopped. It's usually a loose connection or a blocked fan rather than a major failure. A technician needs to check it in person, and we can have someone out to you this week. If the unit is still cooling, it's safe to leave it running until then.

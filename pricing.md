# Pricing: Answerlore

Flat monthly price per company. Not per seat. The number of people sets the tier; it does
not meter the bill. Prices as of 2026-08. Every figure here is generated from
the same file that prints the prices on https://answerlore.com/pricing.

Month to month costs 20% more than the annual rate. The one-time Knowledge Map fee is
charged once, before the subscription starts, and is refunded if the map shows Answerlore
will not help.

## Starter
- Price: $750/month billed annually, $900/month month to month
- One-time Knowledge Map and ingestion: $3,500
- People: up to 10 people
- Product lines or brands: 1 product line
- Knowledge documents: up to 250 documents
- Manual ingestion: 2 manuals a year
- Includes: Cited answers down to the manual page; customer-safe drafts a technician reviews and sends; a ranked gap log; a private instance; export everything any time
- Support: email
- First year, annual: $12,500 (Knowledge Map plus twelve months)
- First year, month to month: $14,300

## Standard
- Price: $1,500/month billed annually, $1,800/month month to month
- One-time Knowledge Map and ingestion: $5,000
- People: up to 30 people
- Product lines or brands: up to 5 product lines
- Knowledge documents: up to 1,500 documents
- Manual ingestion: 8 manuals a year
- Includes: Everything in Starter, plus auto upload (an agent finds the manufacturers' official manuals and ingests them); a quarterly knowledge audit; a monthly gap report. Planned, not built: whitelabel logo, colors, and subdomain
- Support: email plus a quarterly call
- First year, annual: $23,000 (Knowledge Map plus twelve months)
- First year, month to month: $26,600

## Scale
- Price: $3,000/month billed annually, $3,600/month month to month
- One-time Knowledge Map and ingestion: $7,500
- People: up to 75 people
- Product lines or brands: unlimited
- Knowledge documents: unlimited
- Manual ingestion: unlimited
- Includes: Everything in Standard. Planned, not built: Notion and Confluence sync
- Support: named contact, monthly call
- First year, annual: $43,500 (Knowledge Map plus twelve months)
- First year, month to month: $50,700

## Founding-customer pilot
- Price: $750, one-time, invoiced after scope and timing are confirmed
- Length: 30 days
- Scope: 1 product family, 3 manuals (subject to scope review before acceptance), 3 users, 30 evaluation questions
- Includes: Guided onboarding; One end-of-pilot review
- Measures: answer usefulness, source correctness, lookup time, repeated use
- Credit: $750 toward the Knowledge Map setup fee if the customer proceeds after the pilot
- No automatic subscription and no automatic charge at the end of the pilot

## Terms
- Annual price needs a twelve-month term. Month to month cancels any time.
- Outgrowing a tier triggers a notification and a move up at renewal. No overage billing.
- No free tier. No self-serve signup. Access starts with a guided trial: https://answerlore.com/start
- Documents are never used to train models. Full detail: https://answerlore.com/your-data

## Enterprise
Multi-site, SSO, VPC or on-premise deployment, and unusual integrations are quoted
individually. Contact: https://answerlore.com/start or hello@answerlore.com
